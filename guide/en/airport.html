Manas Airport to Bishkek: taxi and minibus 380

Manas Airport is about 25–30 km north of central Bishkek. By car, without traffic, it takes 30–40 minutes.

📅 Checked: 13 November 2026. Prices, opening hours and rules change. Check in 2GIS before you go.

Taxi app (the easiest way)

Locals use Yandex Go and inDrive. You see the price before the ride, and it is usually lower than what drivers in the arrivals hall ask.

The apps need mobile data. You can buy a SIM card right at the airport: how to get a SIM card.

Drivers in the arrivals hall

If you take a car on the spot, agree on the price before you get in: meters are not always used. Compare it with a taxi app first, it takes a minute.

Minibus 380 (cheap but slower)

Minibus (marshrutka) 380 runs from the airport to Bishkek's Western Bus Station. It runs mostly in daytime, so don't count on it in the evening or at night.

You usually pay the driver in cash, so keep small notes. Travellers reported about 50–70 som in 2025, but the fare can change.

What to do right after landing

  • Change a little money or take som from an ATM: money in Bishkek.
  • Buy a SIM card or turn on your eSIM.
  • Install 2GIS: maps, public transport routes and opening hours, all in one app.
