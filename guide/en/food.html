What to eat in Bishkek: Kyrgyz food and drinks to try

Kyrgyz food is hearty and full of meat. Here is what to try in your first days.

📅 Checked: 13 November 2026. Prices, opening hours and rules change. Check in 2GIS before you go.

Dishes

  • Beshbarmak: noodles with boiled meat, the national dish.
  • Lagman: hand-pulled noodles with meat and vegetables.
  • Plov: rice with meat and carrots. It is cooked in the morning and can sell out by evening.
  • Manty: big steamed dumplings.
  • Samsa: a meat pastry baked in a tandoor oven.
  • Kuurdak: fried meat with potatoes and onions.
  • Shorpo: a rich meat soup.
  • Boorsok: fried pieces of dough, served with tea.

Drinks

  • Kymyz: fermented mare's milk, a summer drink.
  • Maksym: a cold drink made from grains.
  • Chalap: a sour milk drink, refreshing in the heat.

Where to eat

The city has many cafes with local food, including chains like Navat and Faiza. For cheap and tasty food, go to Osh Bazaar: samsa, fruit, dried fruit. Keep your bag and phone close.

For vegetarians

In traditional cafes almost everything has meat. It is easier in cafes in the centre: they have salads, vegetable dishes and European menus.
