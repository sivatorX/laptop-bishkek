SIM card in Bishkek for travellers: Beeline, MegaCom, O! or eSIM

Mobile data in Kyrgyzstan is cheap, and in the city all three operators work well: Beeline, MegaCom and O!.

📅 Checked: 13 November 2026. Prices, opening hours and rules change. Check in 2GIS before you go.

Where to buy

At operator shops all over the city and at the counters in Manas Airport. The airport counters are not always open, and some take cash only, so keep a little som.

Bring your passport

SIM cards are registered to a person, so take your passport with you.

Which operator

In Bishkek there is little difference. If you go to the mountains or villages, coverage differs: ask in the shop about the places you plan to visit.

eSIM

You can buy an eSIM online before the trip, so you have data as soon as you land. It is convenient, but usually more expensive than a local SIM.

Free Wi-Fi

Many cafes have Wi-Fi. We collected the ones with fast Wi-Fi where you can work with a laptop on the Laptop Bishkek main page.
