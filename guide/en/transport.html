Getting around Bishkek: buses, marshrutkas, taxis and the Tulpar card

Buses, minibuses and taxis cover the whole city, and the centre is small and green: you can walk to a lot of places.

📅 Checked: 13 November 2026. Prices, opening hours and rules change. Check in 2GIS before you go.

Finding a route

Everyone uses the 2GIS app. It has buses, trolleybuses and minibuses with route numbers and stops.

Buses and trolleybuses: no cash

Since 2025, city buses and trolleybuses are cashless: pay with the Tulpar transport card, a bank card or a QR code in a banking app. The rules have changed several times in recent years, so check when you arrive.

Tulpar cards are sold by drivers, at post offices and at mobile operator shops. Since 2026, the card gives free transfers within an hour.

Marshrutkas (minibuses)

Marshrutkas are small private minibuses. There are many of them, and they go almost everywhere. You usually pay in cash, so keep small notes. Tell the driver in advance where you want to get off.

Taxis

The easiest way is a taxi app: Yandex Go or inDrive. You see the price first, and it is usually lower than a car you stop on the street.

Rush hours

Roughly 8–10 am and 5–7 pm the main avenues (Chuy, Manas, Zhibek Zholu) are busy. In the centre, walking is often faster then.
