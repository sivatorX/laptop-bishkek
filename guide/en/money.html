Money in Bishkek: som, currency exchange, cards and tips

The local currency is the Kyrgyz som (KGS). In the centre you can pay by card almost everywhere, but you will still need some cash.

📅 Checked: 13 November 2026. Prices, opening hours and rules change. Check in 2GIS before you go.

Exchange rate

The National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic publishes the official rate every day. Exchange offices give slightly different rates, so compare a couple nearby.

Where to change money

There are exchange offices all over the centre and in shopping malls. US dollars, euros, Russian roubles and Kazakh tenge are easy to change. Bring clean, undamaged notes: worn or torn ones may be refused or get a worse rate.

Cards

Cafes, shops and malls in the centre usually take cards. Foreign Visa and Mastercard generally work at ATMs and bigger shops, but not everywhere, so keep some cash.

Locals often pay with a QR code in their banking app. Without a local bank account this is usually not an option.

When you need cash

  • Bazaars (Osh Bazaar, Dordoi).
  • Marshrutka minibuses: getting around Bishkek.
  • Small cafes and street food.

Tipping

Many cafes and restaurants already add a service charge to the bill, often 10–15%. Check the receipt before leaving extra.
